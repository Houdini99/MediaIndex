How this works

A link the bots hand to yt-dlp is downloaded with the login stored for its site here — or with Other sites when its site has no entry of its own. Upload a cookies.txt (Netscape format) exported with a browser extension such as “Get cookies.txt LOCALLY” while logged in. Export it from a private window and close that window afterwards: a browser that keeps using the session rotates the cookies and ends the copy you uploaded.

Only the site's own cookies are kept, and they are stored encrypted. After a download that worked, the bots save whatever the site rotated, so a login stays fresh while it is in use. When a link fails with “login required” or “confirm you're not a bot”, upload a fresh export here.
